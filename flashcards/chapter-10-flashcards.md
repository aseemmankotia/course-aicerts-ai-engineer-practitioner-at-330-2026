## Chapter 10: Full AT-330 Practice Exam Simulation & Time-Management Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | AT-330 exam format: total questions and time limit? | 50 questions, 90 minutes. Exam tip: average budget = 1.8 min/question (108 sec); leave 10-15 min buffer for flagged review. |
| 2 | If you spend 60 minutes on the first 40 questions, how much time remains for the last 10? | 30 minutes — about 3 min/question, still within budget since the overall average is 1.8 min/question. Use the extra time on longer scenario questions. |
| 3 | How many AT-330 domains are weighted at 15% each? | Three domains. Exam tip: prioritize review time on these three first — each point of improvement here has outsized score impact. |
| 4 | Recommended minimum time reserved at the end of the exam for flagged-question review? | 10-15 minutes. Exam tip: never use 100% of the clock on the first pass — always bank a review buffer. |
| 5 | Flagging a guessed answer vs. leaving a question blank — which is better and why? | Flag a guessed answer. A blank question guarantees 0 points; a guessed-and-flagged answer has a chance of being correct and can still be revisited. |
| 6 | Reviewing flagged questions in original order vs. by domain weight — which is better time use? | By domain weight. Prioritize the three 15%-weighted domains first to maximize expected score gain per minute of review. |
| 7 | Question phrase '(Select TWO)' vs 'Which is the BEST solution?' — what do these indicate? | '(Select TWO)' = multi-response question, must pick exactly 2. 'BEST solution' with no select count = single-answer question, pick exactly 1. |
| 8 | No explicit 'Select X' instruction appears in a 4-option question — how many answers should you choose? | Exactly one. Absence of select-count language is the default single-answer cue on the AT-330 question format. |
| 9 | What should you extract from a scenario BEFORE reading the answer options? | All explicit constraints: budget, compliance, performance/SLA, timeline. Exam tip: use constraints as an elimination checklist against each option. |
| 10 | Best elimination strategy for 4 plausible-sounding options in a scenario question? | Eliminate options that violate stated constraints first, then compare remaining options against the primary requirement. Neither the most complex nor the most expensive option is a reliable correctness signal. |
| 11 | What typically happens if you fail the AT-330 exam on the first attempt? | A mandatory waiting period applies before retake, and a fee is charged unless a retake voucher is included in the exam bundle. Exam tip: check bundle terms before test day. |
| 12 | Does the AT-330 bundle retake policy typically allow unlimited free retakes? | No. Retakes are not unlimited or free by default — waiting periods and fees (or a limited voucher) apply per standard certification vendor policy. |
