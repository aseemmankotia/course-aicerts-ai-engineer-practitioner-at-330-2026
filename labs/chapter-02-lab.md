# Chapter 2 Lab: The Architecture of Modern AI Systems

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Sketch a three-layer AI architecture for a recommendation engine (using draw.io or pen and paper): data layer with a feature store, model layer with a training job, and serving layer with a REST endpoint

## Key takeaway

Every production AI system decomposes into data, model, and serving layers connected by a feedback loop, and the exam tests this decomposition directly.
