# Chapter 3 Lab: Neural Networks: Perceptrons to Backpropagation

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Build and train a 2-layer neural network from scratch in PyTorch on the MNIST dataset, then plot training vs validation loss curves and identify the overfitting point

## Key takeaway

Backpropagation plus gradient-based optimization is how every neural network learns, and overfitting/regularization tradeoffs are the single most tested concept in this domain.
