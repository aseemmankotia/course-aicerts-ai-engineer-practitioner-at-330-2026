# Chapter 1 Lab: AI Engineering Foundations & Exam Blueprint

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Set up a local Python 3.10+ environment with venv and pip, verify installs of numpy, pandas, scikit-learn, and torch, then run a short script that trains a scikit-learn classifier on the Iris dataset

## Key takeaway

AI engineering treats the ML workflow as an end-to-end pipeline, not just model training, and this holistic view is tested starting in domain 1.
