# Chapter 4 Lab: CNNs, RNNs, and Real-World Neural Network Applications

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Load a pretrained CNN (ResNet18 via torchvision) and classify a handful of sample images, inspecting which layers act as feature extractors vs the classifier head

## Key takeaway

Matching the architecture to the data shape, CNNs for spatial data and RNN variants for sequential data, is the exam's primary test in this domain.
