# Chapter 5 Lab: Transformers, Attention, and What Makes LLMs Powerful

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Use the Hugging Face tokenizers library to tokenize a sample paragraph with a BPE tokenizer, count tokens, and demonstrate what happens when text exceeds a model's context window

## Key takeaway

Self-attention is the core innovation behind transformers, and the exam tests whether you can distinguish pretraining, fine-tuning, and prompting as distinct adaptation strategies.
